# Changelog

## Monoboard 1.1
- **Synthesized Offline Audio & Haptics Engine**: Added 17 procedural PCM sound cues (`WOOD_TAP`, `PIECE_SLIDE`, `DICE_ROLL`, `CARD_FLIP`, `SHUFFLE`, `TILE_MERGE`, `EXPLOSION`, `SPLASH`, `CHECK_ALERT`, `BOUNCE`, `MOVE`, `CAPTURE`, `TURN_PASS`, `HINT`, `KEY_TICK`, `ERROR`, `WIN`) paired with custom tactile haptic waveforms — 100% offline with zero bundled audio bloat.
- **Shared Motion Kit & UI Chrome**: Introduced spring-pop piece placements, bouncy button presses, error/impact shakes, active-target pulses, 3D card flips, animated tumbling dice, animated score counters, confetti victory bursts, and smoother privacy curtain transitions.
- **Board Classics Overhaul**: Added piece spring-pops, check alerts, capture feedback, dice tumbles, and numeric animations across Chess, Checkers, Snakes & Ladders, and Sudoku / Killer Sudoku.
- **Card Games Overhaul**: Added 3D card flip animations, shuffle/draw cues, meld pops, and deal transitions across Klondike, Spider Solitaire, FreeCell, Blackjack, War, Crazy Eights, Go Fish, and Trick-Taking.
- **Classic Casual Overhaul**: Added mark spring-pops, Connect Four disc drops, Battleship explosions & splashes, RPS reveals, Hangman draws, Mastermind peg checks, Dots & Boxes claims, Nim removals, and Memory Match 3D flips.
- **Strategy Games Overhaul**: Added 3D Reversi disc flips, Mancala animated seed counters, Nine Men's Morris mill/shake feedback, Ludo & Backgammon tumbling dice, Go Ko/suicide shakes, Xiangqi check alerts, Shogi drop/promotion bounces, Dominoes & Mahjong mismatch shakes, Chinese Checkers hop bounces, and Carrom pocket ripples with collision clacks.
- **Puzzle Games Overhaul**: Added Minesweeper flood-reveals & mine explosions, 2048 tile color/pop merges, Sliding Puzzle & Jigsaw slot-lock highlights, Nonogram/Kakuro/KenKen completion merges, and Word Search/Crossword/Number Match feedback.
- **Words & Trivia Overhaul**: Added staggered letter-flip reveals in Daily Word, animated rack/pool pops in Word Scramble & Anagrams, low-timer alerts, and sliding question transitions in Trivia.

## Monoboard 1.0
- Initial open-source offline release with 45 classic board, strategy, card, casual, puzzle, and word games, Pass & Play privacy curtain, local AI opponents, and local Room/DataStore persistence.
